How booking works

  1. Get in touch

    Call, email, fill in my booking form, or message me on Instagram or Facebook. Tell me your dog’s breed and when they were last groomed.

  2. Pick a time

    I’m open Tuesday to Saturday, by appointment only. Tell me which days suit you and I’ll find a slot.

  3. Drop off at the salon

    Bring your dog to Four Chimneys, Norton Fitzwarren. I’ll let you know when they’re ready to collect.

Services

Full groom
A bath, blow-dry and brush-out, then a full haircut in the style you’d like.
Bath & brush
A bath, dry and thorough brush-out with no haircut. Good for short coats, or to keep a long coat fresh between full grooms.
Puppy groom
A gentle, unhurried first visit so puppies get used to the bath, the dryer and being handled, ready for grooms later on.

About me

I’m Harri, the groomer behind WolfCuts. My salon is at Four Chimneys in Norton Fitzwarren, a few minutes from the centre of Taunton.

Whether it’s a puppy’s very first bath or a full groom for a fluffy giant, I give every dog the same calm, patient care, so they go home looking and feeling their best.
